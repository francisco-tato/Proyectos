Baking waffles recipe

In this page I will explain how to make waffles.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of flour
  • 2 tablespoons (scoops) of sugar
  • 1 tablespoon (scoop) of baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon of salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 and 3/4 cups of whole or skim milk
  • 1/2 cup of melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Mix in a big bowl all the dry ingredients.
  2. Beat in another container the wet ingredients.
  3. Pour the liquids over the dry ones and mix gently until you get a smooth batter with no lumps.
  4. Heat up the waffle iron and grease the plates with some butter or cooking spray.
  5. Pour a portion of the batter in the center and cook for 4-5 minutes, until golden brown.
