# Project Descriptions

This document provides a brief overview of each HTML page in the repository so the purpose of every project is clear and easy to understand within the wider portfolio.

---

## index.html

This is the main landing page of the portfolio. Its purpose is to welcome visitors, present the overall project collection, and provide easy access to the different pages. It acts as the entry point for the entire portfolio and introduces the body of work in a simple and engaging way.

---

## portfolio.html

This page functions as the personal profile section of the portfolio. It includes information about the creator, their interest in programming, their learning path, and the projects they have built. It also links to certification work, personal projects, and guided exercises, making it a digital portfolio and personal summary in one.

---

## developer-form.html

This page is a survey form designed for students and aspiring developers. It asks questions about coding habits, learning paths, preferred technologies, and personal goals in the world of software development. The structure uses form elements such as text fields, dropdowns, radio buttons, and checkboxes to create a practical HTML exercise.

---

## media-player.html

This page focuses on audio and video playback using HTML elements. It presents media content in a visually clean layout and demonstrates how embedded music and video can be displayed in a web page. The project is a practical exercise in multimedia integration and webpage structure.

---

## event-hub.html

This page works like a sports information hub, highlighting important football events from the future and the recent past. It includes details about competitions such as the World Cup, Copa América, and the UEFA Champions League. The layout is designed to present event information clearly and in an organized way.

---

## spiderman-page.html

This page is dedicated to Spider-Man and explores the character's history, origins, and cultural impact across comics, films, animation, and games. It is a thematic project that combines storytelling with educational content and helps strengthen design and information organization skills.

---

## comic-catalog.html

This page acts as a Spider-Man comic catalog and presents a series of important titles in a structured table. It includes information such as the title, author, genre, and publication year. The goal is to organize relevant material in a clean and readable way, similar to a mini digital archive.

---

## spiderman-rating.html

This page is a movie review focused on "Spider-Man: Brand New Day." It includes a poster image, a brief synopsis, the cast list, and overall review-style content. The project demonstrates how to combine visual elements and text to create a fun and informative entertainment webpage.

---

## General summary

Together, these pages represent a portfolio of web learning projects built with HTML and CSS. Each page has a different theme and purpose, but all of them share the same goal: to showcase creativity, page-building skills, and progression in front-end development.
